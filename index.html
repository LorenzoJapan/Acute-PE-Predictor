About

Pretest probability model

Categorizes presentation as typical, atypical, or severe, then layers alternative-diagnosis judgment and VTE risk factors to reach Low, Moderate, or High pretest probability.1

D-dimer thresholds

A D-dimer <1000 ng/mL rules out PE in low pretest probability; <500 ng/mL rules out PE in moderate pretest probability. High pretest probability proceeds directly to chest imaging.2

Design prototype. This interface demonstrates the published decision logic for interaction-design purposes. It has not been validated as a medical device and should not guide patient care without independent clinical verification and institutional review.
